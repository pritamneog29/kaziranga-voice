About Kaziranga Voice

Kaziranga Voice (kazirangavoice.in) is a web and mobile application designed to amplify your voice in protecting Kaziranga National Park. The application makes it easy to send emails to the Director of Kaziranga National Park expressing your concerns about:

  • Reduction of the Eco Sensitive Zone (ESZ) of Kaziranga National Park
  • Protection of wildlife corridors and biodiversity
  • Preservation of indigenous lands and traditional livelihoods
  • Opposition to development projects threatening the ecosystem (e.g., hotel construction on protected lands)

How It Works

  • Sign in with Google: Securely authenticate using your Google account
  • Compose your message: Draft a personalized email expressing your concerns about Kaziranga
  • Send your voice: Submit your message directly to the Director of Kaziranga National Park
  • Track impact: See the total number of voices raised through this platform
  • Go offline: Draft letters offline, take photos, and track your advocacy activity

Why Kaziranga Matters

Kaziranga National Park is home to the world's largest population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros and numerous other endangered species. Recent threats to its Eco Sensitive Zone and indigenous lands pose serious risks to biodiversity and communities that depend on these ecosystems. Every voice matters in conservation efforts.
